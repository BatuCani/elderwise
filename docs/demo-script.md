# Demo video script (2 minutes)

Record the phone screen (iPhone: Control Center → Screen Recording, with the microphone on) and talk over it.
One person speaks, one person holds the phone. Do one full rehearsal, then record. Keep the best take.

## Before you record
- Open https://elderwise-beryl.vercel.app on the phone, Profile: language **English**, text size **Normal**, speaking speed in the middle.
- Answers tab: **Clear history**, so the demo starts empty.
- Phone volume up, silent mode off (so "read aloud" is heard), notifications off (Focus mode).
- Do one test question first, so you know the live app and the API key work right now.

## Script

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 - 0:15 | Title or the Talk screen | "880 million people are over 65. AI could help them every day, but almost none of them use it: every AI app expects you to read dense screens and type well. And scammers already use AI against them." |
| 0:15 - 0:25 | Talk screen: "How can I help?", the big orb | "This is Elderwise, with Grace, a patient AI helper. One button: tap once, then just say what you need. Meet Ingrid, 78." |
| 0:25 - 0:45 | Tap the orb and ask out loud: **"Is this message real?"** The last words appear very big while she speaks. Tap **Start camera** → "Try an example" → **Parcel text message** → **Done talking** | "Ingrid got a text: her parcel is held, she should pay a small fee via a link. She asks out loud, like she would ask a person, and sees every word she says, big, so she knows Grace is listening. Then she shows Grace the message." |
| 0:45 - 1:10 | Answers: the deep red **scam alert**, read aloud | Let it read aloud for a few seconds, then: "Claude reads the photo and her question. One clear answer, three simple steps, in a calm voice." |
| 1:10 - 1:25 | Tap **Tell my daughter Anna** → the ready-written message → **Send Anna a message** (the SMS app opens) | "And the most important part: one tap, and her daughter Anna knows. The message is already written for her." |
| 1:25 - 1:40 | Back on Answers, scroll to **You learned**, then open the **Learn** tab | "Every answer teaches one AI skill. And in Learn, short lessons she can try with Grace right away. That is how Ingrid learns AI: by using it." |
| 1:40 - 1:52 | Profile: text size **Extra large**, then **Deutsch**; show Talk again | "Made for older eyes and ears: bigger text without breaking the screen, slower speech, English or German." |
| 1:52 - 2:00 | Talk screen | "Elderwise. AI that adapts to people, not the other way round. Built today with Claude." |

## If something goes wrong
- No microphone (plain http, or denied): type the question in the bar above the tab bar instead. It works the same.
- Claude is slow: keep talking over the "thinking" glow; cut the wait in editing if you can.
- The SMS app opens on a real phone: that is fine, show it for a second and go back. Don't send it.
- Wrong or odd answer: just record again. Use the same example photo; it gives the most reliable demo.
