# Grace - finals script (5 minutes, live demo)

Live app: https://graceapp.vercel.app
If the mic fails: tap "+" then "Type" and type the question.

## 0:00 - Hook (slide: Ingrid)

Hi, we're team Grace.

This is Ingrid. She's 78, and last week she got this text:
"Your parcel is waiting. Pay a 1.99 euro customs fee."

She almost paid.

Older adults are the number one target for scams, and most of them have never used AI, because apps assume you can read tiny screens and type fast.

So we asked: what if AI could be patient?

## 0:40 - Meet Grace

This is Grace. You show it something, you ask out loud, it explains it in plain words, and it teaches you one AI skill every time.

## 1:00 - Live demo

One question, one big button, and a friendly face that listens.

(Tap the mic:) "I got a text from DHL. I have to pay 1.99 euro. Is this real?"

(Card appears.) "Don't pay this text." Three simple reasons: DHL never asks for money by text, the address is fake, small fees steal card details.

(Tap "Tell my daughter Anna".) One tap, and a message to her daughter is ready.

(Tap "+", "Scan", photograph a letter or receipt.) A pension letter full of numbers. Grace highlights the date, the amount and what to do, and explains it like a friend.

(Open "Learn", then "Profile".) And Grace is a teacher. Every use teaches a real AI skill, and Ingrid sees her progress. She even picks her assistant's personality and voice.

## 3:30 - Why it matters

Y Combinator lists "AI for the Aging Population" as an open request for startups. Families are worried, and Grace gives them peace of mind: scam alerts, a family view, a one-tap call.

## 4:00 - How it works

Claude is the brain: it reads images, uses tools and writes simple, safe answers. Gemini Live gives Grace a real-time voice. It's a web app, live at graceapp.vercel.app, no download.

Built today by Batu, Sam, Maike, Ahmet and Endrik.

## 4:40 - Close

No one should be left behind by AI just because they're older.

Grace. AI that is patient with you.

Thank you.
