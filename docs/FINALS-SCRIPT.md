# Grace - finals script (6 minutes, live demo, no video)

Live app: https://graceapp.vercel.app
Fallback if the mic fails: tap "+" then "Type" and type the question.

## 0:00 - Hook (slide: Ingrid's photo)

Hi, we're team Grace.

This is Ingrid. She's 78, she lives alone in Berlin, and last week she got this text message:
"Your parcel is waiting. Pay a 1.99 euro customs fee."

She almost paid.

Older adults are the number one target for scams.
And while everyone in this room talks about AI every day, most people like Ingrid have never used it, because apps assume you can read tiny screens, type fast and know what to ask.

So we asked: what if AI could be patient?

## 0:45 - Meet Grace (slide: logo)

This is Grace.
You show it something, you ask out loud, and it explains it in plain words, and it teaches you one AI skill every time you use it.
Let me show you.

## 1:15 - Live demo (phone, mirrored)

This is the home screen. One question, one big button, and a friendly face.
That little character is Grace's assistant. It listens, thinks and talks with you.

(Tap the mic and speak:) "I got a text from DHL. It says I have to pay 1.99 euro. Is this real?"

Grace listens, and Claude, our AI brain, checks it.

(Answer card appears.)
A big red card: "Don't pay this text." Three simple reasons: DHL never asks for money by text, the web address is fake, and small fees are used to steal card details.

And here's the part we love.
(Tap "Tell my daughter Anna".)
One tap, and a message to her daughter is ready. Ingrid isn't alone with it anymore.

(Tap "+", then "Scan", photograph a letter or a receipt.)
Now a letter from the pension office, full of numbers and official words.
Ingrid just takes a photo.
Grace reads it, highlights what matters - the date, the amount, what to do - and explains it like a friend would.
It even checks receipts and payment QR codes.

(Open "Learn", then "Profile".)
But we don't want Ingrid to depend on us. Grace is a teacher.
Every time she uses it, she learns a real AI skill: show a photo and ask, check if something is real, ask for simpler words.
Here she sees her progress, and there are short lessons for scams, letters, health and her phone.

(In Profile, tap a personality - the bot hops.)
And Ingrid chooses who she talks to: a patient helper, a cheerful friend, someone short and clear, or a storyteller. Even the voice and the speed.

## 4:15 - Why it matters

Y Combinator lists "AI for the Aging Population" as one of its open requests for startups.
Soon one in five people will be over 65, and their families are worried.
With Grace the family gets peace of mind: scam alerts, a simple family view, a one-tap call.

## 5:00 - How it works

Claude is the brain: it reads images, uses tools and writes simple, safe answers.
Gemini Live gives Grace a real-time voice.
It's a web app, live right now at graceapp.vercel.app. No download, it works on any phone.

We built this today, five people: Batu, Sam, Maike, Ahmet and Endrik - builders, design, and people who know exactly who Ingrid is.

## 5:40 - Close

Our goal is simple: no one should be left behind by AI just because they're older.

Grace. AI that is patient with you.

Thank you.

## Likely questions

- Privacy? Nothing is stored without an account; contacts stay on the phone; accounts use Supabase with row-level security.
- What if the AI is wrong? Grace explains and always points to the doctor, pharmacist or a trusted person for health and money.
- Business model? Free for seniors; paid by families, care services or health insurers.
- Why web and not an app? No install, works on every phone, can be added to the home screen like an app.
